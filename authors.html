Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take a percentage of my book royalties?

No, absolutely not. We operate entirely on a flat-fee service model. You retain 100% of your copyright, 100% of your distribution rights, and 100% of your royalties. When you publish your book using our files, all sales revenue goes directly into your personal merchant account.

Who owns the final book cover and interior layout files?

You do! Once final delivery is complete, you own all the final publication-ready files outright. You can use them across any platform, make updates, or distribute them however you choose without needing our permission or paying additional licensing fees.

How does the book submission process work, and who uploads it?

We handle the technical setup by logging directly into your account to submit and publish your book. We do not publish your book under our corporate accounts because we believe in complete transparency: we take zero percentage of your royalties and zero cuts of your sales. By publishing directly inside your own account, you retain full ownership and control, meaning you can easily make minor changes or updates in the future without needing to ask us for permission.

How do distribution choices affect my paperback and book platforms?

When we set up your book, we will guide you through your distribution options:

  • Paperback Extended Distribution: For paperbacks, we can select multiple distribution platforms through Amazon so that Amazon handles a wider network of distribution channels.
  • Other External Platforms: If you want your book distributed on specific external platforms that are not covered by Amazon’s standard distribution network, you can create your account for those platforms, and we will log in to upload it for you.
  • Note on Royalties: Selecting multiple platforms can impact your paperback royalty splits compared to keeping distribution restricted or exclusive.
What is the review and approval timeline, and is it guaranteed?

The standard review and approval time for books submitted to platforms like Amazon is typically 7 working days. However, please note that platforms operate independently, and we cannot guarantee whether a platform will accept or decline a book, as final publishing approval is entirely at the sole discretion of each platform’s content guidelines and review teams. We ensure your files are formatted to professional industry standards to give you the highest chance of smooth approval.

Do I need to buy my own ISBN, or do you provide it?

We’ve got you covered. We provide the ISBN directly as part of our publishing service packages. We register and assign the ISBN for both your print and digital formats, and we generate the exact barcode required for your book’s back cover design.

What trim sizes and cover finishes do you support?

We support industry-standard professional trim sizes, such as 5.5" x 8.5" (ideal for fiction and memoirs) and 6" x 9" (ideal for non-fiction and business books). For your cover, you can choose between a professional Matte finish (smooth, modern, and non-reflective) or a Glossy finish (vibrant, shiny, and high-contrast).

How do I communicate with my team, and how do I track my revisions?

We keep communication streamlined, secure, and fully organized. Every author has a dedicated project record in our system so nothing gets mixed up. Furthermore, after every phone call or meeting we have, our team sends a detailed written summary email outlining your requested changes and next steps so you always have a clear record.

How many revisions do I get on my interior layout and cover design?

Our workflow includes dedicated review stages where you can review your first draft and cover concepts. We gather your notes clearly and apply interior layout and cover adjustments according to your package guidelines. We ensure you are fully satisfied before signing off on final delivery.

What exact files will I receive at the end of the project?

You will receive a complete, publication-ready digital package tailored for independent publishing. This includes:

  • A print-ready formatted interior PDF (configured with proper margins, gutters, and page numbers).
  • An optimized EPUB file for eBooks (KDP and reader-ready).
  • A high-resolution full cover spread PDF (with spine calculation and ISBN barcode).
  • High-resolution standalone eBook cover artwork (plus your 15 custom illustrations if you chose the Children’s Book package).
Can I use AI to help write or create my book, and what do the platforms require?

Yes, major publishing platforms like Amazon KDP do allow AI-assisted and AI-generated content, but they require strict transparency and disclosure during the publishing upload process. Here is how platforms distinguish the two:

  • AI-Generated Content (must disclose): If an AI tool was used to generate actual text, chapters, translated text, or cover/interior artwork and illustrations, you must disclose it when publishing, even if you applied substantial edits afterward.
  • Our role: We format and prepare the files you provide. You are responsible for ensuring that any AI-generated text or images you choose to use comply with platform guidelines and copyright requirements.
