Our services and packages

We offer author and self-publishing services, video editing and post-production, and website redesign and maintenance. Package contents and prices are described on this website. The exact scope of your project is confirmed in your project brief before work begins.

Pricing and payment

  • Prices are flat fees as listed on this website, shown in US dollars, and are confirmed before work starts.
  • The Monthly Growth Plan for website redesign has no upfront build fee, is billed monthly, and has a 3-month minimum commitment.
  • The One-Time Complete Package for website redesign is a single payment.
  • Additional work outside the agreed scope, such as extra full-page illustrations at $30 per page, is quoted and agreed before we start it.
  • Payment schedule and accepted payment methods are confirmed in writing before work begins.

  • Provide complete, accurate materials and information, and respond to review requests in a timely way.

  • Follow the rules of any platform you publish on. If you use AI tools to create text, images or illustrations, you are responsible for disclosing this where a platform requires it, and for ensuring your content meets platform guidelines and copyright requirements.
  • Keep any account access you give us secure and remove it when the project is complete.

Reviews, revisions and approvals

Our projects include review stages so you can give feedback before final delivery. Feedback is gathered through written notes, timestamps or an agreed review tool, and revisions are applied according to your package guidelines. Revision rounds are confirmed before work begins. Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately.

We do not take any percentage of your book royalties or sales.

Choosing multiple distribution options can affect your paperback royalty splits.

Delivery times depend on the size and complexity of your project and how quickly we receive materials and feedback. Any timeline we give is an estimate. For example, most website redesigns are drafted, reviewed and launched within 1 to 2 weeks of receiving your updated business information.
