From First Cut to Final Delivery

A clear, collaborative process with your review and approval at every stage.

  1. Project Brief & File Intake

    We learn about your goals, audience, and style. You share your footage via Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, or WeTransfer.

  2. Rough Cut

    We organize the footage, select the strongest takes, and assemble the initial edit focusing on narrative flow and pacing.

  3. Review & Feedback

    You review the rough cut and provide feedback using timestamps or an agreed review tool.

  4. Fine Cut & Sound Design

    We refine the edit, improve transitions, clean up dialogue, and balance music and sound effects.

  5. Color Grading & Graphics

    We apply the agreed color treatment and add required titles, captions, branding, and visual effects.

  6. Final Export & Delivery

    After final checks, we export your video in the confirmed file format, resolution, and aspect ratio.

Video Editing Questions

How do I send you my footage?

You can share your video files through Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io, WeTransfer, or another agreed file-sharing service.

What editing software do you use?

Our workflow includes Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and Adobe After Effects, depending on project requirements.

Can you add music and sound effects?

Yes. We incorporate suitable music and sound effects based on your creative direction, using appropriately licensed assets.

How do revisions work?

You provide feedback through timestamps and an agreed review method. Revision rounds are confirmed before work begins.

What video resolution and file formats will I receive?

We deliver final videos in formats and resolutions suited to your project, commonly MP4 and ProRes.

How quickly can you deliver my video?

Turnaround depends on video length, footage volume, and complexity. A realistic timeline is established after reviewing your requirements.

Can you edit videos for multiple platforms?

Yes. Depending on the package chosen, we can prepare versions for different platforms, including vertical, square, and widescreen formats.

Can you work with existing branding and creative references?

Yes. You can provide your logo, brand colors, fonts, and style references to maintain brand consistency.

Website Redesign Questions

Can you redesign my site without losing my current domain name or email accounts?

Yes. We handle the technical launch carefully to ensure your domain name, email accounts, and existing online links stay completely active and uninterrupted during the switch.

Will my new website work well on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site we build is mobile-first. Your phone number, address, and booking links will be front-and-center so phone users can contact you immediately.

What counts as an included update or maintenance task?

Small updates include changing office hours, updating staff or doctor photos, swapping out text, tweaking service descriptions, or adding a new announcement. Major changes (like creating an entirely new sub-brand) are scoped separately.

Can you connect our current online booking tool or patient portal?

Yes. If you use a third-party booking system or patient portal, we can easily embed or link it directly into your new website buttons.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do, 100%. You retain full ownership of your domain name, images, text, and content.

How long does a website redesign take?

Most redesign projects are fully drafted, reviewed, and launched live within 1 to 2 weeks after receiving your updated business information.
