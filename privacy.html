Who we are

Clarity Digital House (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides book publishing support, video editing and website redesign services. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and while delivering our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We keep what we collect to what we need to answer you and do the work.

  • Contact details you send us, such as your name, email address and the details of your project, when you use our contact form or email us.
  • Project materials you choose to share, such as manuscripts, artwork, footage, logos, brand guidelines, and website content.
  • Account access you grant so we can submit or update work on your behalf, for example a publishing dashboard or website hosting login.
  • Communication records, including the written summary we email after calls or meetings.
  • Basic technical data that your browser sends to any website, such as IP address and device type, which our hosting provider may log for security and reliability.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and prepare project proposals.
  • To deliver the services you have asked for, including formatting, editing, design and submission work.
  • To keep a clear written record of requests, feedback and approvals.
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your project materials for anything other than your project.

Account access and your files

For publishing and web projects we work inside your own accounts so you keep full ownership and control. We only use the access you give us for the tasks you have agreed to. We recommend using a unique password or a delegated-access option where the platform offers one, and changing credentials once your project is complete. We never publish your book under our own accounts.

Who we share information with

We share information only where needed to deliver your project or run our business:

  • Service providers such as email, file-transfer, review and project-tracking tools (for example Google Drive, Dropbox, Frame.io or WeTransfer, when you choose to use them).
  • Platforms you authorize, such as Amazon KDP, when we submit files through your account at your request.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where required by law.

These providers have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them.

Cookies and third-party content

This website does not use advertising trackers, and its fonts are hosted on our own site. If we add analytics or other third-party tools in future, we will update this policy first. Any images or files loaded from outside services may share your IP address with those services.

How long we keep information

We keep project records and communications for as long as needed to deliver your project, support you afterwards, and meet legal or accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your project materials and personal information at any time, and we will do so unless we are legally required to keep something.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including limiting access to people working on your project. No method of transfer or storage is completely secure, so please share sensitive login details only through channels we have agreed on.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal information, and to object to certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services and this website are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are publishing a children’s book, we only need information about you, the author, and the book itself.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email hello@claritydigitalhouse.com.
