Frequently Asked Questions

How do your flat-fee and pricing models work?

Every service operates on a clear, upfront flat fee or straightforward monthly arrangement. We do not take cuts of your book royalties, and we do not bundle hidden upgrade fees into our projects.

Do I retain ownership of my work, files, and accounts?

Yes, entirely. Whether we format your book, edit your footage, or redesign your website, you own 100% of the final deliverables, source files, and copyright. For publishing and web design, we work directly inside your own personal accounts so you maintain full operational control.

How do we communicate and handle project feedback?

We keep communication organized and secure. You receive structured updates at every milestone, and following any project call, our team sends a detailed written summary email outlining your feedback and next steps so there is always a clear paper trail.
